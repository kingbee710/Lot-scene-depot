How this board works

  1. Fill in the questionnaire and agree to the rules. Name, handle, why you want in, contact (optional), tick that you agree to the rules — that's it, no Facebook, no proof.
  2. You're in — no approval wait after that. Once you've done the questionnaire and agreement, you post and comment with just your handle, right away.
  3. Break the rules and you're warned, then banned. If you post gross/vulgar stuff, bigotry, political stuff, or violence, you'll be asked to stop / warned. Do it again after a warning and you will be banned — posts taken down, no more posting.

What belongs here

Open — and it does not have to be lot-related: events, ideas, stories, something that happened to you, funny rants, networking, partnering up, and services. This is a real-life community board, meant to bring people together — food drives, somebody giving out stuff at Christmas to people who are homeless, community help, things like that. Advertisements are allowed as long as they're related to the counterculture.

What does not belong here

No vulgar or wacky stuff, nothing gross or offensive, no bigotry, no political content, and no violence. If you do one of those things, you'll be asked to stop / warned. If you do it again after you've been warned, you will be banned — your posts come down and you can't post again.
