How this board works

  1. Apply to join. Fill in the Join form below. You are not a member until WooKzZz approves you.
  2. Approved members post and comment. Just use your approved handle — no token to copy. Every post and comment still waits for owner approval first.
  3. Nothing goes public on its own. Every post and comment waits at The Gate for owner approval first.

What belongs here

Open — and it does not have to be lot-related: events, ideas, stories, something that happened to you, funny rants, networking, and comments on posts. Ideas like “I wish we could create something like this” and “what if we as people did this differently” belong here.

What does not belong here

No vulgar or wacky stuff, nothing gross or offensive, no homophobic content, no political content, and no violence. Posts that break those rules are not approved.
